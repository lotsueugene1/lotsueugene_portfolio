## Research question

AI-generated image detectors are often evaluated on clean benchmark data, while images in the real world are compressed, resized, cropped, blurred, noised, and captured through screenshots. I investigated how well a CLIP-based detector holds up after those transformations.

## Experimental design

I designed and implemented a PyTorch and scikit-learn pipeline around CLIP ViT-B/32. The study covered ten transformations at five severity levels and completed more than 120,000 image evaluations.

## Evaluation

Detector behavior was measured with AUROC, F1, precision, recall, and accuracy. Tracking each metric across increasing severity made it possible to distinguish gradual degradation from sharper failure points.

## Outcome

The project quantified robustness gaps that clean-data evaluation can conceal. It was recognized among the Top 100 submissions in the National Student Research Institution Summer Research Hackathon 2026.

## Tools

Python, PyTorch, scikit-learn, OpenAI CLIP, NumPy, Pandas, and reproducible experiment pipelines.
