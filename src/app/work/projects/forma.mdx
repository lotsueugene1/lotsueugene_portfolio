## Overview

I engineered Forma, an open-source, production-grade form builder inspired by Typeform and Google Forms. It supports more than 18 field types, including payments, file uploads, bookings, and ratings. Docker provides consistent development environments and scalable deployment.

## Conversational forms

I designed and implemented a one-question-at-a-time form engine to create a focused experience that improves usability and form completion.

## Payments and subscriptions

Stripe Connect enables payments and subscriptions, allowing creators to monetize forms and collect payments within submission workflows.

## AI form generation

Amazon Bedrock with Claude Haiku converts natural-language prompts into fully configured forms, reducing the work required to create complex form structures.

## Automation and integrations

I implemented email automation with Resend and real-time integrations with Slack, Google Sheets, and webhooks for end-to-end submission workflows.
