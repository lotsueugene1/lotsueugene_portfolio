## Overview

At VECINTI, I architected and shipped a production marketplace for booking local services. The platform brings scheduling, resource allocation, location-aware discovery, payments, and AI-assisted operations into one system built with Next.js, TypeScript, and PostgreSQL.

## Booking and discovery

The booking engine supports six service types while accounting for time zones, concurrent cart holds, and staff or resource availability. PostGIS powers geospatial search, and a six-signal ranking system turns location and quality signals into useful results.

## Agentic operations

I built an AI system on Amazon Bedrock with 35 tools. Permissions are scoped, and state-changing actions require human approval. Calendar, email, SMS, 13 scheduled jobs, and 13 signed webhooks connect the agent to real operational workflows.

## Payments infrastructure

Stripe Connect supports provider payouts, subscriptions, identity verification, refunds, disputes, and event-driven processing across more than 21 webhook event types.

## Engineering focus

This work required careful coordination across time, money, location, and automation, where small inconsistencies quickly become user-facing failures. The architecture emphasizes explicit state transitions, secure integration boundaries, and reliable background processing.
